Extreme heat and people in need

A live update of Extreme heat: Preparing for the heatwaves of the future (OCHA, IFRC and the Red Cross Red Crescent Climate Centre, 2022), recomputed with current weather data and applied to the people in need in this year's humanitarian plans.
